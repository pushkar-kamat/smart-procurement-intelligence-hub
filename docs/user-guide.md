# User guide
## Requester
Sign up/confirm email or use the provided demo identity. Open New requisition, select department and add one or more items. Enter specification, quantity/unit and estimated price. Save draft; edit before submission. Submit to procurement. Draft/submitted requests can be cancelled with a reason; later states cannot be changed by requester. You can view only your own requests/documents.

## Procurement
Maintain active vendor records on Vendors. Open a submitted request and record invitations. Quotations tab: enter a complete offer for each invited vendor, including date, delivery, unit prices, tax percentage and currency discount. Attach PDF/PNG/JPEG evidence before approval; document attachment is immutable. An incorrect document requires a fresh procurement record and audit explanation, not silent replacement.

Comparison shows unit prices, calculated totals, delivery, flags/explanations and risk factors. Mark comparison ready, choose preferred vendor and record why. Lowest total and risk score do not choose a winner. Any quote edit before approval resets comparison readiness. Submitted approvals lock prices. After all approvals, issue the PO and print its page.

## Approver
Open Approval inbox, inspect items, Comparison and Audit tabs. Approve or reject with a comment. High-value requests need a different finance approver next. You cannot approve your own request. Rejected workflows are terminal; requester creates a revised request.

## Finance
Approve senior-level requests when required. Record deliveries or verify procurement's delivery. After full delivery, record the invoice. Check mismatch warning, attach invoice document and record a meaningful final review before closing. Closure cannot be undone through the UI. No payment processing occurs.

## Interpreting intelligence
FLAGGED means the statistical or configured price boundary was exceeded; it is not proof of overcharging. Risk points combine historical rates; it is not a prediction of fraud. Insufficient history explicitly requests human review. Compare specifications and unit consistency before relying on the reference price.
